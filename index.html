Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Dustfall ("the Game", "we", "us") is a 2D platformer developed by Daniel Herrero Pardo. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Dustfall — on Google Play, in a web browser, on itch.io or anywhere else — and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Dustfall has no user accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally, on your device only (browser localStorage or the app's own storage):

  • the run in progress: the random seed and the input log needed to replay it;
  • your best distance and other local records;
  • your settings, such as language and accessibility options;
  • your unlock progress, including cards bought with the in-game coin wallet;
  • a random device identifier, used only for the optional global record described below.

This information never leaves your device unless you are part of the optional global record. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing the app's data or your browser's site data, deletes all of it.

3. The optional global record

Dustfall can show the best distance reached by any player. If that feature is enabled, when a run settles the Game may send one anonymous entry to our server: the random device identifier described above and the distance you reached. It contains no name, no email address and no advertising identifier, and it is never used to identify you. The Game is fully playable offline; with no connection it simply skips the submission, and nothing read from that record ever changes how the simulation plays.

4. Advertising

Dustfall may offer optional rewarded ads — for example, to redraw the upgrade draft. Ads are opt-in and the Game works without them. When you choose to watch one, the ad is served by a third-party ad network: Google AdSense or Google Ad Manager in the browser build, and Google AdMob in the mobile build. Those networks may collect device information and identifiers, such as the advertising ID, and may use cookies or similar technologies, as described in Google's own policies. We do not receive your personal information from them.

5. Permissions

The mobile version requests only what it needs to run and, if ads are enabled, to load them. Dustfall does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

6. Children

Dustfall is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Everything described in section 2 lives on your device and you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data. The anonymous global-record entry is kept only so the global record can be displayed; you can ask us to remove it by writing to the address in section 9.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when a new feature changes what the Game handles. The effective date at the top always reflects the latest version, and material changes will be reflected in the Game's store listing.
